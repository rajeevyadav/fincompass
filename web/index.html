Forecast — probability of beating the S&P 500

A calibrated probability that a US stock outperforms the S&P 500 over the horizon, from the same Bayesian reference model as the desktop app — it runs entirely in your browser. Not a target price, expected return, or advice.

One-time setup: the Forecast needs daily prices, which browsers can't fetch from Yahoo directly. Paste your free Cloudflare Worker URL (see the 5-minute guide). Stored in your browser only.

Three-stage DCF (with reverse DCF)

Values a company from its free cash flow: five high-growth years, a five-year glide to a stable rate, then a perpetual terminal. Enter the figures you have; every number is yours and stays in your browser.

Option pricing (Black-Scholes) + Greeks

European call/put value, the Greeks, and a profit/loss-at-expiry diagram. The premium shown is the model value from your inputs.

Bond analytics

Price, yield, and interest-rate sensitivity. A Treasury yield is a risk-free reference — a corporate bond needs a credit spread on top, so enter the actual required yield.

Two-asset portfolio risk

How much a two-holding mix swings, and where the risk comes from. Volatility is how much the value bounces around — not how much it will gain or lose.
