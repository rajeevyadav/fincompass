# FinCompass — Disclaimer

FinCompass is an educational research tool. It is **not financial, investment, tax, legal, accounting, or brokerage advice**, and nothing it displays is a recommendation or solicitation to buy, sell, hold, or trade a security.

FinCompass has two different model outputs:

1. **Evidence-score probabilities/intervals** describe uncertainty in the internal FinCompass evidence score. They are not future-return probabilities.
2. **Forecast probabilities**, when a validated real model is installed, estimate only the exact binary event, horizon, benchmark, hurdle, feature protocol and dataset/model version recorded in that model's manifest.
3. **Live/adaptive probabilities**, when permitted by the adaptive gate, are bounded updates around a validated frozen anchor using timestamped new information. Candidate adaptive values may be displayed while warming, but a correction is not applied unless the adaptive state satisfies its documented prequential gate and source-freshness controls.

A `validated_research` or `validated_market` frozen-anchor label means the artifact satisfied the documented FinCompass validation protocol and data-quality conditions. It does **not** guarantee future calibration, profitability, suitability, absence of bias, or protection against regime change.

Market and fundamental data can be delayed, incomplete, inconsistent, revised, throttled, incorrectly normalized, or wrong. Free-provider definitions and corporate-action treatment can differ. FinCompass does not independently audit issuer statements or guarantee provider accuracy.

Forecast probabilities do not represent expected returns, target prices, certainty of profit, or individualized recommendations. Transaction costs, taxes, liquidity, capacity, borrow availability and portfolio-level risks may be outside the model.

You are responsible for independent verification and any financial decisions. Investing can result in partial or total loss of capital.

Near-real-time source checks are best-effort and may be delayed, stale or unavailable. A successful prior provider check does not prove that no newer information exists. FinCompass exposes freshness/staleness controls and can fall back to the frozen anchor, but it cannot guarantee complete real-time market awareness.
